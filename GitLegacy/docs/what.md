Here's a complete list of things you can extract and keep as memories. I'll group them so it's easy to decide what matters to you.

## 📊 Numbers & Stats (the "by the numbers" stuff)

- Total commits across all repos
- Total lines of code added / removed
- Number of repositories you contributed to
- Number of pull requests opened / merged
- Number of code reviews you did
- Number of issues opened / closed
- First commit date & last commit date (your "tenure")
- Longest commit streak
- Most active day / month / year
- Average commits per week

## 📅 Time-based Patterns

- Commits per year (4 bars showing your journey)
- Commits per month (heatmap or line chart)
- Commits by day of week (are you a Monday warrior or Friday hero?)
- Commits by hour (early bird vs night owl)
- Your contribution graph screenshots (the green squares) for each year

## 🏆 Highlights & Proud Moments

- Top 10 biggest commits (by lines changed)
- Top 10 PRs you're most proud of (titles, descriptions, links)
- Features you shipped (list them out)
- Bugs you squashed that you remember
- Major refactors or migrations you led
- First commit ever (the nostalgic one)
- Any commit messages that make you laugh now

## 👥 Collaboration

- People you reviewed code with the most
- People who reviewed your code the most
- Teams/projects you worked with
- Mentees or juniors you helped (if you remember)

## 📁 Code Footprint

- Top 20 files you touched most often
- Languages you worked with (Python: X%, JS: Y%, etc.)
- Folders/modules you "owned"
- Tests you wrote (count of test files)

## 🎨 Visual Keepsakes

- Screenshot of contribution graph (each year)
- Screenshot of profile page
- Screenshot of any badges/achievements
- A custom "stats poster" combining everything
- Wordcloud of your commit messages (fun!)

## 📝 Personal Touches

- Your most-used commit message words
- Funniest commit messages
- Most dramatic commit message ("FINALLY FIXED IT 😭")
- A timeline of major projects you worked on
- Personal notes — what each year felt like

## 🎁 Final Output Ideas

Things you could create from all this data:

1. **A stats poster** — like a Spotify Wrapped but for your career
2. **A PDF report** — "My 4 Years at \[Company\]"
3. **An HTML page** — interactive, with charts
4. **A simple text file** — minimalist, just the numbers
5. **A photo album** — screenshots organized by year

⚠️ **What NOT to extract** (important):

- Actual source code
- Internal documentation
- Customer data or names
- Proprietary algorithms
- Private repo contents
- Company secrets / API keys / configs

Stick to _metadata about your work_, not the work itself.
